## 4.

> "Explain what probate is, what triggers it, what funds does it cover."

Probate in Ontario is a court procedure that authenticates Mary's will and authorizes Daughter as Estate Trustee. The court issues either a Certificate of Appointment of Estate Trustee (estates over $150k) or a Small Estate Certificate (≤ $150k). Probate is triggered when a third party — typically a bank or land registrar — won't deal with the executor without the certificate. It covers only assets in the estate base; joint-with-survivorship and named-beneficiary assets (like the RIF) pass outside. The *Pecore* characterization determines whether the joint assets fall inside or outside that line. Detail in `findings/probate-and-eat.md`.
